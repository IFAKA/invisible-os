# Stranger test: 30 minutes, 5 people

Goal: find out if a person who never saw Invisible OS can set it up and use it with the screen off.

## Who
- 5 people. Mix: 2 sighted, 2 low-vision or blind if you can find them, 1 older person.
- They use Android. Not a developer. Never saw the app.
- Test one person at a time. About 30 minutes each.

## Before you start
- Test phone: Android 10 or newer, wired or Bluetooth earbuds with one button.
- Install the app. Do NOT turn on any access.
- Put WhatsApp (or another supported chat) on the phone. Have a second phone ready to send a test message.
- Put 3 songs in YouTube Music or another music app.
- Have a timer or clock. Have a notes sheet (the table below). Ask permission to record the screen and sound (no faces). Say that you test the app, not the person.

## Say this (and nothing more)
"This app lets you use the phone with one earbud button and no screen. It speaks the options. You click when you hear the one you want. I will not help. If you are stuck, say so out loud. Please think out loud."

## Part 1: setup without help (10 minutes max)
Give the phone and the earbuds. Say: "Please make the app work so you can use it with the screen off."
Watch: do they find the setup card? Do they turn on Button access, Notification access, and Assistant? Do they understand the warning screens?
Help only after 10 minutes. Then write "helped" and what you said.
Success: all three on, and the screen off, and the earbud button makes the phone speak.

## Part 2: five tasks (3 minutes each, 15 minutes total)
Screen off for every task. Phone in the pocket or on the table.
1. Hear new messages. You send a WhatsApp message to the phone. Say: "A friend wrote to you. Find out what it says."
2. Reply to it. Say: "Answer yes."
3. Play music. Say: "Play some music."
4. Open an app through the tree. Say: "Open the Clock (or Calculator) app."
5. Leave. Say: "You are done. Stop the app."
Stop a task at 3 minutes. Write "failed".

## What to watch and write down
- Time to finish each task (seconds).
- Number of wrong clicks and wrong menu items.
- Did they hear and understand the speech? Did they click too early or too late?
- Do they know where they are (menu, list, reply)?
- Do they use the undo? Do they ever try to speak to the phone? (There is no microphone.)
- Exact words they say when confused. Write them as spoken.
- Anything they were afraid to press.
- Do they look at the screen? When?

## After (5 minutes)
Ask:
1. What was the hardest part?
2. What did you like?
3. Would you use this? When? (Pocket, walking, driving, cooking, in bed.)
4. Did you trust it to send the right reply?
5. One thing to change?
Score trust and ease from 1 to 5.

## Success bar
The test passes if all are true:
- At least 4 of 5 finish setup alone in 10 minutes.
- Each task is done by at least 4 of 5 people with no help.
- Median time: hear message under 30 s, reply under 45 s, music under 45 s, open app under 60 s, leave under 20 s.
- No one sends a wrong reply without a chance to undo.
- Average ease and trust are 4 or more.

If setup fails for 2 or more people, fix setup first. Nothing else matters if people cannot turn it on.
Group the problems. Fix the ones that hit 2 or more people. Test again with 5 new people.
